Exam anchors from Mid and Final exams appear here. Plans are built day by day up to the exam window.

Difficulty

Gemini scores each topic from 1 to 10. 1 means easy review, 5 means moderate, and 10 means very hard or concept-heavy.

Preference

0 means low preference, 5 means neutral, and 10 means highly preferred. Lower preference pushes the topic earlier so the hard or less-liked material gets attention sooner.

File support

PDF, slides, Word documents, images, and plain text can all feed the topic estimate.

Planning rule

Priority = Difficulty × 2 + (10 - Preference). Missed days can be pulled forward again with one click.
